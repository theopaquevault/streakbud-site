Streakbud Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Welcome to Streakbud (the "App"). We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

Our Core Commitment: Streakbud is a privacy-first habit tracking tool. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect, store, or upload your personal habit data. All of your habit information (names, check-in records, notes, etc.) is stored locally on your device. If you choose to enable iCloud sync, your data will be stored in your personal iCloud account, which we cannot access or view.

Please read this policy carefully before using the App. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, upload, or share with any third party:

  • Personally identifiable information (such as name, email, or phone number).
  • Your habit data, check-in records, or notes entered in the App.
  • Precise location information.
  • Health, financial, or other sensitive personal information.

1.2 Subscription Management (RevenueCat)

The subscription features of the App are powered by RevenueCat. When you purchase or manage a subscription, RevenueCat collects necessary purchase information, including purchase records and subscription status. This information is used solely to verify your subscription entitlement and provide related service features. RevenueCat does not collect your payment information (such as credit card numbers) or sensitive data such as health or location.

RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

1.3 Information We Automatically Collect

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash reporting tools. Therefore, we do not automatically collect your device information, usage statistics, or crash logs.

2. Data Storage

  • Local Storage: All of your habit data is stored locally on your device by default.
  • iCloud Sync: You may choose to enable iCloud sync in the App settings. Once enabled, your data will be synced to your personal iCloud account and stored and encrypted by Apple Inc. under its iCloud terms. We cannot access, view, or control your iCloud data.
  • Retention Period: Your data will remain on your local device or in your iCloud account until you delete it in the App or uninstall the App from your device.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

Information processing may be involved only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: The App integrates RevenueCat, which collects only necessary purchase information and cannot be used to identify you personally.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, we may need to cooperate in providing information. Since we generally do not hold your personal data, you may need to provide such information from your own device.

4. Your Rights

Since we do not collect your personal data, you have full control over your data:

  • Access and Export: You can view and export all your habit data within the App.
  • Deletion: You can delete individual records in the App or clear all data in the settings.
  • iCloud Data: To delete synced data from iCloud, go to iOS Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage, find Streakbud, and delete its data.

5. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If significant changes are made, we will notify you via in-app pop-ups or release notes. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically to stay informed.
